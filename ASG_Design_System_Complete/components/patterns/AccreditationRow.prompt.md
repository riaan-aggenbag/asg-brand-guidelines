# AccreditationRow

Footer proof strip on the ink ground: bordered pills for memberships, brass-bordered pills for review counts. Sits above the footer's bottom bar.

```jsx
<AccreditationRow
  pills={["Cape Town Tourism", "SATSA", "ATTA Corporate", "TripAdvisor Certificate of Excellence"]}
  reviews={["TripAdvisor · 1,000+ reviews", "Feedback Company · 1,000+ reviews", "Google · 400+ reviews"]}
/>
```
