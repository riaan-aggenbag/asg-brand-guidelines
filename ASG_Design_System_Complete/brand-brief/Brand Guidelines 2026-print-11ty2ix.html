Brand Guidelines 2026Introduction

Introduction

A brand built on quiet confidence

African Safari Group is a Premier Inbound Travel Agent, designing and fulfilling five-star safari journeys across Southern and East Africa — sold through consultation, never self-service.

Our brand is dark, restrained, editorial luxury — closer to Aman or Singita than a typical travel agency. Confidence is shown through precision and space, never volume. This guide exists so that every touchpoint — a letter, a business card, a website, a newsletter — feels unmistakably like us.

Please treat it as the single source of truth. When in doubt, choose restraint: fewer colours, more space, one clear message.

"Specific over superlative. Never salesy, never shouting."

Our voice in one line.

How to use this guide

Sections 01–04 define the fixed foundations — logo, colour, type, voice. Section 05 shows how they come together across the materials you'll produce most: stationery, cards, folders, websites and newsletters. The foundations never change; the applications show you how to apply them.

The four principles

Restraint

Space and silence do the work.

Precision

Specific detail over adjectives.

Warmth

Authoritative, never cold.

Consistency

One brass, one crest, everywhere.

01 · The LogoLockups

The Mark

The crest & primary lockups

Our mark pairs a fixed lion crest with a tracked serif wordmark. The crest is never redrawn. Use the lockup that fits the space — and always give it room to breathe.

Stacked — the master lockup

Horizontal — preferred for nav & headers

Crest only — favicons, stamps, tight spaces

Ink version — for light grounds

One mark, three lockups. Choose by context, not preference. The stacked lockup leads on covers and formal documents; the horizontal lockup runs in website and letterhead headers; the crest alone appears only where the full name already reads nearby.

01 · The LogoClear space & size

Protection

Clear space & minimum size

Minimum clear space = the height of the crest, on all sides

Always surround the logo with clear space at least equal to the height of the crest. Keep type, images, edges and other logos out of this zone.

Minimum sizes

Stacked lockup32 px / 12 mm
Horizontal lockup28 px / 24 mm wide
Crest only20 px / 8 mm

Below these sizes the crest detail breaks down. When a mark must go smaller than the minimum, use the crest alone rather than the full lockup.

02 · ColourThe 60 / 30 / 10 rule

Proportion

The 60 / 30 / 10 rule

Every layout follows one proportion. Use brass like brass fittings in a lodge — signal, never ground.

60%
30%
10%
60%
Ink & Charcoal — the dominant dark ground carrying most of the surface.
30%
Ivory & White — breathing room, light editorial areas, negative space.
10%
Brass — one primary CTA, key numerals, active states, the rule-line.
Do
  • Let ink and ivory carry the layout
  • Reserve brass for a single point of emphasis per view
  • Use generous negative space as a design element
Don't
  • Fill backgrounds or large blocks with brass
  • Use multiple accent colours competing for attention
  • Crowd the layout — density reads as cheap

03 · TypographyTypefaces

Two typefaces

Lora & Poppins

Serif — display & headlinesLora
Where lions still rule
Regular 400Medium 500Semibold 600Italic

One serif moment per screen. Short, confident headlines of six to nine words. Italic — often in Heritage Sand — for emphasis.

Sans — body, UI & all numeralsPoppins
Three nights, R 14,500
Regular 400Medium 500Semibold 600Bold 700

Body copy, navigation, captions — and all numerals, even inside serif paragraphs (Poppins Medium, tabular figures). Prices in Rand: R 14,500.

Only these two typefaces. Never substitute Arial, Times, or a decorative font. If Lora or Poppins is unavailable in an app, use the nearest system serif / sans and correct it in the final artwork.

04 · Voice & ImageryVoice & tone

How we write

Voice & tone

We write like the specialist a client is grateful to have found — authoritative, warm, restrained. Confidence shows through precision, never volume.

Instead of

"Experience the adventure of a lifetime on this dream safari!"

Write

"Sabi Sand has the highest leopard-sighting density in Africa. Three nights gives you the best odds."

Principles

Specific over superlative. Name the lodge, the season, the sighting odds — not "unforgettable" or "dream".

Declarative sentences. Short, certain statements.

A person, not a funnel. Consultation-led, never transactional. Lead with what the guest experiences.

Mechanics

"We" and "you". First person plural for us; address the guest directly.

No exclamation points. Calm confidence outperforms enthusiasm.

No emoji, ever. Numerals carry weight — prices, night counts, reply times, sighting percentages.

04 · Voice & ImageryPhotography

Imagery

Photography direction

Photography carries more of this brand than any single design element. The direction is cinematic and restrained — one clear subject, real negative space, a warm, slightly desaturated grade.

Golden-hour wildlife
one clear subject
Wide environmental lodge
with negative space
Do
  • Shoot at golden hour, one clear subject
  • Leave real negative space in frame
  • Keep a consistent warm, slightly desaturated grade
  • Place any text over a dark bottom-up scrim
Don't
  • Use flat midday light or crowded grids
  • Use generic OTA / stock imagery
  • Over-saturate — neon grass, orange HDR skies
  • Cram text over busy detail

Quick referenceDo & Don't

At a glance

The essentials

Always
  • Hold to 60 / 30 / 10 — ink, ivory, brass
  • Use brass sparingly, as a signal
  • Keep the crest fixed & give it clear space
  • Set headlines in Lora, everything else in Poppins
  • Write specific, calm, declarative copy
  • Choose restraint and negative space
  • Cite accreditations on formal materials
Never
  • Use brass as a large background
  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or shadow the crest
  • Introduce a third typeface or new colours
  • Use bright / neon colours or heavy gradients
  • Use exclamation points or emoji
  • Set long body copy on dark grounds
  • Crowd a layout — density reads as cheap

"When in doubt, choose restraint — fewer colours, more space, one clear message."
