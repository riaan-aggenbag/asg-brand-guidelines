Overview

Perched above the Bushman’s River valley

Set against a cliff above the Bushman’s River valley in the malaria-free area of the Amakhala Game Reserve, this family-run lodge boasts two outdoor pools and all-inclusive meals. Each stone-and-thatch lodge has a private deck with panoramic views of the game reserve, is air-conditioned and has an en-suite bathroom, connected by stone walkways tucked into indigenous trees.

Guests enjoy meals at the on-site restaurant, plus a high tea with homemade breads and cakes. Savour a drink at the pool overlooking the Amakhala plains or around the campfire. Two game drives are included for every night of your stay. Addo Elephant National Park is 50 km away.

Best Time to Visit

When to go

Game viewing is rewarding year-round and malaria-free. Dry winter is peak for sightings; green summer brings lush scenery and newborn animals.

Dry Winter

May – September

Animals gather at the water, the bush thins out and viewing is excellent. Cold mornings, minimal rain and few mosquitoes. Peak game viewing.

Green Summer

October – March

Lush landscapes, newborn animals and migratory birds. Warm days of 25–32 °C (77–90 °F) and busier periods over the SA school holidays. Year-round and malaria-free.
