About the Reserve

A living wilderness, restored by its founders

Amakhala is a privately owned reserve in the Eastern Cape, formed when seven neighbouring farms dropped their fences to restore the land to its original wilderness. Today it spans 9,000 hectares of rolling bushveld, river valleys and the Bushman’s River, and is home to all of the Big Five.

Year-round and malaria-free, with five distinct ecosystems, it is ideal for first-time safari-goers, families with young children, and anyone pairing a reliable Big Five experience with the Garden Route.

Best Time to Visit

A year-round, malaria-free safari

Being malaria-free, Amakhala welcomes guests in every season. Your perfect time depends on whether you prefer crisp winter sightings or lush summer landscapes.

Dry Winter

May – September

Animals gather at the water and thinner bush opens up the views, making for excellent, reliable game viewing. Expect cold mornings, minimal rain and few mosquitoes. Peak game viewing.

Green Summer

October – March

Lush landscapes, newborn animals and migratory birds fill the reserve. Warm days of 25–32 °C (77–90 °F) and busier periods over the South African school holidays.

Getting There

Easy to reach, worlds away

Just 72 km (about 75 minutes) from Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) Airport, with daily flights from Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban — no long transfers or bush flights required to reach true Big Five country.

1

Fly to Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha)

The airport receives daily flights from Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban — just 72 km (about 75 minutes) from the reserve.

2

Transfer & open with a sundowner drive

Fly in, transfer in the late afternoon, and start your safari with a sundowner game drive on the evening you arrive.

3

Self-drive or add to your route

A paved road runs to the gate, making Amakhala self-drive friendly — and an easy add-on to the Garden Route or Addo Elephant Park.
