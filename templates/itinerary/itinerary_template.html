03 · Destinations

Two landscapes, one journey

Private bushveld on the western edge of the Greater Kruger, then the escarpment and the Sabie Valley.

Moditlo Private Game Reserve

11 Dec 2024 – 13 Dec 2024

Nestled near Hoedspruit, Moditlo Private Game Reserve is a captivating escape adjacent to the renowned Greater Kruger National Park and Blue Canyon Conservancy. Pronounced ‘mo-di-tlo’, the reserve's name pays homage to its majestic elephant herds across 36,000 acres of African bushveld. Moditlo boasts lakes, streams, and dams that nourish the growth of lush vegetation, offering a perfect habitat for an array of wildlife. Set against the backdrop of the Drakensberg mountain range, Moditlo offers a rich array of wildlife, from hippopotamus and cheetah to giraffe, zebra, and more. With African Wild Dogs, diverse birdlife, and over 50 bushveld tree species, it's a haven for nature enthusiasts. Enjoy thrilling safaris, game drives, hot air balloon rides, and an immersive experience in the heart of untamed Africa.

Hazyview

13 Dec 2024 – 14 Dec 2024

Surrounded by subtropical fruit orchards, Hazyview is a small farming town and a gateway to the southern part of the Kruger National Park and the Panorama Escarpment. The town and its spectacularly scenic surroundings, serve as a popular adventure capital with a remarkable array of exhilarating outdoor activities on offer to suit all ages, tastes and fitness levels. These include, among others: mountain biking, quad biking, ziplining, tubing, abseiling, and river rafting. Whether you are eager to hike the Panorama Route with its breathtaking views and impressive waterfalls, visit the Shangana Cultural Village, or horse ride along the banks of the magnificent Sabie River, Hazyview has something for just about everyone.

Foothills of the Drakensberg, Sabie Valley

Chestnut Country Lodge in Hazyview, Mpumalanga, offers a serene getaway nestled in the foothills of the Drakensberg mountains. The Lodge had its beginning in 1996, when an old farm was purchased by the first owners. Many decades later this property has been transformed and expanded into a beautiful country lodge boasting 13 rooms, a restaurant, a swimming pool, and a terrace with breathtaking views of the Sabie Valley.

The lodge features a variety of accommodations, including comfort deluxe rooms, luxury rooms, and the Chestnut Suite. Each option offers an en-suite bathroom and amenities such as ceiling fans, air-conditioners, bar fridges, tea- and coffee-making facilities, and patios, all with garden & valley views.

Guests can enjoy facilities such as a restaurant, bar, swimming pool, and uncapped Wi-Fi, but the most praised quality of this property is most definitely it's tranquil setting that overwhelms the guest with nothing but the peaceful sounds of nature.

The lodge is ideally located between the Kruger National Park and Panorama Route, making it a perfect base for exploring local attractions and outdoor activities.

Expert tips

  • Travel Insurance: Don't travel without travel insurance. You never know what can happen while traveling so it's best to be prepared for anything. Also, post-Covid, lodges have dramatically changed their cancellation policies and penalties, and most offer no refunds, only a date change (as is the general rule with flights).
  • Travel water bottle: Comes in exceptionally handy around Africa on your game drives / nature walks.
  • Camera Gear: Chances are you'll want a camera for your trip to Africa. Remember lots of memory cards / storage options!
  • Safari Clothes: Lightweight, beige / light greens / browns / earthy colours, and moisture-wicking clothing are great for traveling. Also, bring a warm jacket and pants for the early morning and evening game drives.
  • Safari Hat: A good wide-brimmed hat is both necessary and functional.
  • Binoculars and sunglasses: You will be very glad that you brought these.
  • Safari Pants: We recommend neutral-coloured pants as they're great at hiding dirt and can match most shirt colours.
  • Safari Shirt: Lightweight, and remember that full sleeves will keep the bugs away!
  • Boots / Comfortable sneakers: While you don't need to wear sturdy shoes every day, at least one pair of safari / hiking boots will make your trip more enjoyable.
  • Travel Adapter: You'll need a special travel adapter for traveling to Africa. Get one before you get there so you don't pay a premium on the ground.
  • Mosquito spray / roll-on stick – Most lodges supply this but please pack your own just in case.

08 · Information

South Africa

One of the most culturally and geographically diverse places on earth, South Africa, fondly known by locals as the ‘Rainbow Nation’, boasts 11 official languages, and its inhabitants are influenced by a fascinating mix of cultures. Discover the gourmet restaurants, impressive art scene, vibrant nightlife, and beautiful beaches of Cape Town. Enjoy a local braai (barbecue) in the Soweto township, browse the bustling Indian markets in Durban, or sample some of the world's finest wines at the myriad wine estates dotting the Cape Winelands. Some historical attractions to explore include the Zululand battlefields of KwaZulu-Natal, the Apartheid Museum in Johannesburg, and Robben Island, just off the coast of Cape Town. Above all else, its untamed wilderness is astonishing: wildlife roams freely across massive unfenced game reserves such as the world-famous Kruger National Park.

Local cuisine

South African cuisine is diverse and flavourful, featuring dishes like biltong (dried, seasoned meat), bobotie (spiced minced meat baked with an egg-based topping), and boerewors (farmers' sausage). The culinary scene includes a strong tradition of barbecuing, known locally as "braai."

Drinks

Notable local drinks include Rooibos tea, craft beers, and South African wines.

Tipping

Customarily 10-15% in restaurants and for services.

The best time to visit South Africa is from May to September for wildlife viewing, as animals are more concentrated around water sources. For beach holidays, mid-December to March is ideal, especially in Cape Town.
