Overview

Four tents, the river, and the plains beyond

HillsNek is an intimate tented camp on the banks of the Bushman’s River, tucked into a quiet corner of the malaria-free Amakhala Game Reserve. Just four private safari tents stand elevated on wooden decks, each with panoramic views of the plains — game viewing can often be enjoyed from the comfort of your bed.

The main lodge of stone, canvas and thatch is the heart of the camp: shared dinners under the African sunset, a bar, a pool overlooking the bush, and BBQ evenings on the deck. Families are genuinely welcome — the Luxury Tent has its own adjacent children’s bedroom, and kid meals and babysitting can be arranged. The camp’s founders also established the Chipembere Rhino Foundation, and conservation runs deep here.

Questions

Frequently asked

How many tents does HillsNek have?

Just four private safari tents, each elevated on a wooden deck with panoramic views of the plains — one of the smallest camps in the Eastern Cape.

Are game drives and meals included?

Yes — all meals and two guided game drives a day (early morning and late afternoon) are included. Your consultant will confirm drink inclusions for your dates.

Do the tents have air conditioning?

No — as a tented camp, the tents have no air-con. Fans keep summer comfortable; heaters, electric blankets and even hot water bottles on cold-morning drives handle winter.

Is HillsNek good for families?

Genuinely. The Luxury Tent has an adjacent children’s bedroom with two singles, the Family Tent sleeps four, and kid meals, board games and babysitting (extra) are available. Children 9+ are welcome.

Is HillsNek malaria-free?

Yes. Amakhala lies in the Eastern Cape's malaria-free zone, so the camp requires no antimalarial medication — a real advantage when travelling with children.

Will we see lions?

Lions live on the reserve and sightings are frequent, though never guaranteed — staying two nights or longer gives your rangers the best chance.

Is there a spa or hot tub?

No spa centre and no hot tubs — massage treatments can be arranged during your stay. The pool deck with its view does a lot of the relaxing for you.

Can we watch game from the tent?

Often, yes — the tents face the plains, and game viewing can be enjoyed from the deck or even from bed.

When is the best time to visit?

Year-round. Winter (May–September) is driest and best for big-game sightings; summer (October–March) turns the plains green and fills them with young animals and 250+ bird species.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 13:00 and check-out by 11:00. Early or late times can be requested when you book, subject to availability.

How do we get there from Port Elizabeth?

Chief Dawid Stuurman International Airport is about 70 km away — roughly an hour by road via the N2. Transfers can be arranged.

What is the Chipembere Rhino Foundation?

A rhino conservation foundation started by the camp’s founders — proceeds and awareness from stays support anti-poaching and rhino protection on the reserve.
