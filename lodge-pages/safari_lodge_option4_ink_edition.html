Overview

Slow down to the rhythm of the wild

On the northern boundary of the malaria-free Amakhala Game Reserve, this owner-run lodge invites you to settle into the rhythm of the wild — birdsong at dawn, distant lion calls at dusk, the quiet movement of antelope through the underbrush. Fine African bead work and handcrafted detail run through the lodge, and the suites and lounge look out over a waterhole where giraffe, antelope and elephant come to drink.

Eleven luxury suites each carry a private plunge pool and hot tub. The compact reserve keeps wildlife close: tracking the Big Five from an open 4x4, a giraffe silhouetted against the sunset, or a slow cruise on the Bushman’s River. On stays of three nights, a morning visit to Addo Elephant National Park can be arranged.

Questions

Frequently asked

Are game drives included in the rate?

Yes — two guided game drives a day are included, along with accommodation and meals. Your consultant will confirm the exact inclusions for your travel dates.

Is Safari Lodge malaria-free?

Yes. The entire Amakhala Game Reserve sits in the malaria-free Eastern Cape, so no antimalarial medication is needed — one reason the region suits families and longer stays.

Will we see lions?

Lions live on the reserve and sightings are frequent, though never guaranteed — staying two nights or longer gives your rangers the best chance of finding them.

Can we drive ourselves on game drives?

No — all game drives are guided, in an open safari vehicle with a qualified ranger. Night drives with spotlights can also be arranged.

Do the suites have private plunge pools?

Yes. Each of the 11 Luxury Suites has its own private plunge pool and hot tub, plus a patio, air conditioning, minibar and mosquito net. Suites are 323 m².

Is there a spa at Safari Lodge?

There is no dedicated spa centre, but in-suite wellness treatments — massages, facials, manicures and body treatments — can be arranged at an additional charge.

Can we visit Addo Elephant National Park?

Yes. On stays of three nights, a morning game drive into nearby Addo Elephant National Park — home to one of the world’s densest elephant populations — can be arranged.

When is the best time to visit?

Game viewing is rewarding year-round. Dry winter (May–September) is peak for sightings as animals gather at water; green summer (October–March) brings lush scenery, newborn animals and migratory birds.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 13:00 and check-out by 12:00. Early or late times can be requested when you book, subject to availability.

Are children welcome?

Children aged 9 and older are welcome. Guests 12 and over are charged as adults; cots and extra beds are not available. Two children aged 9–11 currently stay free on the kids special.

Is there a restaurant and bar?

Yes — the lodge has its own restaurant and bar, with room service available. Breakfast is a consistent guest favourite, with vegetarian and vegan options.

How do we get there from Port Elizabeth?

Chief Dawid Stuurman International Airport is 76 km away — about an hour via the N2 towards Makhanda, then the R342 Paterson turn-off to the signposted lodge entrance. Transfers can be arranged.
